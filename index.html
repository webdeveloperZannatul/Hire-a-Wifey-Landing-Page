FAQ

Quick answers

Everything you need to know. Still unsure? Send us an enquiry — we reply within hours.

Ask us anything
What’s included in a Wifey visit?
Cleaning, laundry, ironing, bed making, dishes, light organisation and meal prep — you choose the mix. We bring a checklist and tailor it to your home.
Do I get the same Wifey each time?
Yes, where possible. We know trust matters, so we prioritise consistency. If your Wifey is away, we’ll arrange a vetted backup.
Are you NDIS and DVA approved?
We work with NDIS participants and DVA clients regularly, with clear invoicing for plan managers and providers. Enquire and we’ll guide you.
What areas do you cover?
All of Hervey Bay: Urangan, Torquay, Scarness, Pialba, Eli Waters, Kawungan, Wondunna, Urraween and surrounds. Not sure? Ask us.
Do I need to provide cleaning products?
We can use your preferred products, or bring our own on request. We’re careful with surfaces and always ask about allergies or preferences.
